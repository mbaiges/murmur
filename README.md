# Murmur 🌊

> *The world's noise, distilled into one quiet sentence. Every hour. On your desktop.*

Murmur is a Windows desktop application that fetches RSS headlines from a curated list of news sources, synthesizes them through Google Gemini AI, and renders the resulting poetic phrase as your wallpaper.
